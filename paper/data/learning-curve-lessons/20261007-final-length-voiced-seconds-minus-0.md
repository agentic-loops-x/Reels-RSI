Final length = voiced seconds minus ~0.5 s per crossfade overlap: a 92-character script (25.2 s voiced at ~3.7 字/秒) over 5 frames rendered 25.2 s for a 30 s target. Count characters as (target + 0.5 × frames) × 3.7 before writing SCRIPT.md, not target × 3.7.
