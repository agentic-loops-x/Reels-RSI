Map-frame camera focus points are projected PIXEL coordinates (WK.P(lon,lat)), not lon/lat or guessed numbers: write the cam() focus as WK.P(...) of the place being explained. Guessed focus values in a first build aimed every frame's camera at empty parchment while lint and check passed.
