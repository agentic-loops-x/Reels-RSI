Map frames with a large top-left hero word (150-200 px) and world-space river/city labels: both content_overlap errors in a map reel were a label sitting under the hero at some camera state. Pick label coordinates from the projected map AFTER choosing the hero box (x<720, y<330 reserved at every camera state) and place NW-region labels on the far side of their river, not at its centre.
