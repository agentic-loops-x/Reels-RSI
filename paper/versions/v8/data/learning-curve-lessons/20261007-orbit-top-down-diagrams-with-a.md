Orbit/top-down diagrams with a small orbiting body (r about 55 px) make a hero that reads tiny in the contact sheet even though the ring is large: pair it with a screen-stable inset of the viewer's-eye result at >= 400 px, and start the camera at scale >= 1.1 rather than 1.0.
