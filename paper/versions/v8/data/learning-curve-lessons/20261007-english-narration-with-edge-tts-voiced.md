English narration with edge-tts voiced ~3 words/s and frames got ~20% shorter than the planned duration after 'reels voice' (a 32 s plan became 24.9 s); SFX cues and shot time-codes written before voicing ended up past the frame end. Budget ~3.1 words/s when scripting, and write sfx/shot times only after reading the frame's real duration and word times in the packet.
