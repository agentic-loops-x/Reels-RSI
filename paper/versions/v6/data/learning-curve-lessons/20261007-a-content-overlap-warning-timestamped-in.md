A content_overlap warning timestamped in the first ~0.5 s of a frame (or last 0.5 s of the previous one) is two frames' labels overlapping during the crossfade, not a defect in either frame: snapshot that timestamp before editing, and don't chase it by moving labels that are fine in isolation.
