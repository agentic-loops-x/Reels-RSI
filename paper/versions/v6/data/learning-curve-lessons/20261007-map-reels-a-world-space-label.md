Map reels: a world-space label or caption-sized pin that is only 'static' (always visible) still gets positioned by the camera's START state — pins for places outside the early view (e.g. a far-south city in a frame that starts zoomed north) land in the caption band or under the title plate in the first seconds. Give each pin a reveal time after the camera has moved into view, or omit it, and compute its screen y at t=0 as well as at the end.
