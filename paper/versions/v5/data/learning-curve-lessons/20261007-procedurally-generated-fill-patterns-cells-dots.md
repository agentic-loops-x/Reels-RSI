Procedurally generated fill patterns (cells, dots, tiles) inside a curved container must be tested against the container outline with a margin: a grid cell centred inside an ellipse still poked its corner across the leaf edge; lint and check pass, only the contact sheet shows it. Use an inset ellipse (about 0.85 of the outline) or test all four corners.
